**🎼 Characteristics**

- **Contrapuntal Interplay**: A lively and intricate two-part invention where both hands share equal importance in mimicking and chasing the main motif.
    
- **Expressive Key**: Written in B minor, carrying a slightly serious but highly energetic and forward-moving character.
    

**🎯 Learning Objectives (For Portfolio)**

- **Hand Independence**: Developing the ability to maintain equal clarity and dynamic balance while both hands play complex, independent 16th-note passages.
    
- **Rhythmic Precision**: Keeping a strict, steady pulse without rushing, especially during long streams of continuous notes.
    
- **Articulated Execution**: Mastering a crisp, detached, yet singing tone (cantabile) suitable for the Baroque style, avoiding blurry overlapping notes.
    
- **Leap Accuracy**: Executing awkward intervallic jumps and wide spans smoothly while maintaining correct hand posture.
